Billder Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 10, 2026

Billder ("we", "our") is a bill-splitting app. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it's handled. If you have questions, contact us at ricardoromeuluiz@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

Account & profile. You sign in using Google or Apple Sign-In — we do not have or store passwords. From your provider we receive your email, name, and (for Google) a profile picture. We also store your default tip preference and default payment method type.

Payment information. Card details are entered directly into Paystack's secure hosted checkout page — Billder never sees or stores your full card number or CVV. We store only a Paystack-issued payment token and non-sensitive card metadata (last 4 digits, card brand, issuing bank, expiry) so you can reuse a saved card. We also store transaction records: amounts, fees, status, and Paystack's transaction reference.

Bill session data. When you create or join a bill session, we store the items, prices, quantities, and how they're split or assigned among participants. If you upload a photo of a receipt, it is processed using Google Cloud Vision (to read the text) and an AI language model provider (to structure the extracted items and totals). Other participants in the same bill session can see your name, profile picture, and what you've been assigned or have paid.

Support requests. If you contact support, we log your IP address, browser/device user-agent, and the request details to help diagnose issues.

Crash and diagnostic data. We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and diagnostic logs (device model, OS version, app version, stack traces) to help us fix bugs. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not collect precise location or contacts data.

2. How we use your information

  • To operate bill sessions: creating, joining, splitting, and settling shared bills
  • To process payments through Paystack
  • To extract itemized data from receipt photos you upload
  • To provide customer support
  • To keep the service secure and diagnose technical issues

3. Sharing with third parties

We share data with service providers strictly to operate the app:

  • Paystack — payment processing
  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple — authentication
  • Google Cloud Vision — optical character recognition of uploaded receipts
  • OpenAI — structuring receipt text into itemized data
  • Microsoft Azure — hosting and infrastructure monitoring
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — crash reporting and diagnostics

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data retention

Bill sessions with no completed payments are deleted once they end. Once any payment has been completed in a session, the paid transaction records, participant history, and session details are retained for our accounting and support records. Support request logs are retained only as long as needed to resolve the issue.

5. Your choices

You can update your profile preferences (tip default, payment method) at any time in the app. You can delete your account and associated data directly in the app, under Profile settings. Deletion is subject to records we're required to retain (e.g. completed payment transactions) for legal or accounting purposes.

6. Children's privacy

Billder is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
